## reminders.when

Read a natural time or place phrase the way reminders_add will; stores nothing.
EN/DE, calendar anchors ('after the concert', '2h before my flight') and places ('when I get home').

Input:
- `phrase` (string, required): e.g. 'tomorrow 9', 'friday 18:00', 'in 2 hours', 'morgen früh', 'after the concert', '2h before my flight', 'when I get home'

Output: `kind`, `at`, `on`, `why`, `form` (concise leaves out form)
Example: `tool call reminders.when '{"phrase": "tomorrow 9"}'`
Example: `tool call reminders.when '{"phrase": "when I get home"}'`

## reminders.list

Pending reminders with their IDs (timed, series, place), soonest first.
mine = only his; query filters the text; recent adds his ones fired in the last 36 h.

Input:
- `mine` (boolean): only his reminders (owner + place ones), not my notes
- `query` (string (optional)): only reminders whose text contains this (any case)
- `recent` (boolean): also his reminders fired in the last 36 h (for snooze/done)
- `limit` (integer, default 50)

Output: `now`, `count`, `items`, `recent`
Example: `tool call reminders.list '{}'`
Example: `tool call reminders.list '{"mine": true, "query": "dentist"}'`

## reminders.add

Add a reminder: his (owner=true, Telegram at the minute by cron) or a note for me (owner=false).
Give when (a time or phrase) or on (a place). every repeats; call also rings him.

Input:
- `text` (string, required): what to remind (his words, short)
- `owner` (boolean, required): true = his reminder (Telegram at the time); false = a note for me (inbox)
- `when` (string (optional)): 'YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM' (home wall time) or a phrase ('tomorrow 9', 'friday 18:00', 'in 2 hours', 'after the concert'); a place phrase ('when I get home') becomes `on`
- `on` (string /^(home|leave|(leave:)?zone:\S.*)$/ (optional)): place trigger instead of a time: home | leave | zone:NAME | leave:zone:NAME (his reminders only)
- `every` (string (optional)): repeat rule (timed only): daily | weekdays | weekends | weekly[:mon,thu] | monthly[:DAY|last] | yearly | every:Nd | every:Nw
- `until` (string /^\d{4}-\d\d-\d\d$/ (optional)): last day of a series (with every)
- `every_time` (boolean): place only: fire each time, not just next time
- `urgent` (boolean): place only: fire in quiet hours too
- `call` (boolean): also ring him at the time (wake or departure call; his only)
- `soft` (boolean): my nudge to him: waits out a sleep-in morning
- `tag` (string /^[A-Za-z0-9_.:-]{1,40}$/ (optional)): machine key for cancel by tag
- `via` (telegram | voice | app (optional)): who asked: telegram | voice | app (analytics)

Output: `id`, `kind`, `owner`, `at`, `local`, `every`, `until`, `on`, `where`, `call`, `message` (concise leaves out message)
Example: `tool call reminders.add '{"text": "call the garage", "owner": true, "when": "tomorrow 9", "via": "telegram"}'`
Example: `tool call reminders.add '{"text": "water the plants", "owner": true, "when": "2026-11-02 18:00", "every": "weekly"}'`

## reminders.snooze

Snooze a reminder (pending or fired in the last 30 days) by its ID: it comes back at the new time.
A second snooze moves the same entry again; next = a place reminder's next time there.

Input:
- `id` (string /^[0-9a-f]{8}$/, required): the reminder's 8-hex ID (reminders_list, or the button data)
- `when` (string, required): +1h | +Nm | +Nh | +Nd | tonight | tomorrow | next (a place reminder's next time) | 'YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM' | a phrase

Output: `id`, `at`, `message`
Example: `tool call reminders.snooze '{"id": "1a2b3c4d", "when": "+1h"}'`
Example: `tool call reminders.snooze '{"id": "1a2b3c4d", "when": "tomorrow"}'`

## reminders.move

Move a pending one-off reminder to a new time (not a series: cancel or skip that).

Input:
- `id` (string /^[0-9a-f]{8}$/, required): the reminder's 8-hex ID (reminders_list, or the button data)
- `when` (string, required): new time: 'YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM', +2h, tomorrow or a phrase ('friday 18:00')

Output: `id`, `at`, `message`
Example: `tool call reminders.move '{"id": "1a2b3c4d", "when": "friday 18:00"}'`

## reminders.cancel

Cancel reminders: one by id (a series goes whole), or all with a tag, or all whose text matches.

Input:
- `id` (string /^[0-9a-f]{8}$/ (optional)): one reminder (a series goes whole)
- `tag` (string /^[A-Za-z0-9_.:-]{1,40}$/ (optional)): every reminder with this tag
- `match` (string (optional)): every reminder whose text contains this (any case)

Output: `cancelled`, `lines`
Example: `tool call reminders.cancel '{"id": "1a2b3c4d"}'`
Example: `tool call reminders.cancel '{"tag": "bill:3"}'`

## reminders.press

Run a reminder button (rm:s1h|ston|stom|snxt|done:ID): +1 h, tonight 19:00, tomorrow 09:00, next time, done.

Input:
- `data` (string /^rm:(s1h|ston|stom|snxt|done):[0-9a-f]{8}$/, required): a reminder button's callback data, e.g. rm:s1h:1a2b3c4d

Output: `reply`
Example: `tool call reminders.press '{"data": "rm:s1h:1a2b3c4d"}'`

## reminders.fire

The hub timer's call at a reminder's due instant: remind fires it if its store still holds that slot.

Input:
- `id` (string /^[0-9a-f]{8}$/, required): the reminder's ID (8 hex)
- `at` (string /^\d{4}-\d\d-\d\d \d\d:\d\d$/, required): the slot it is due at, home wall time

Output: `id`, `at`, `decision`, `mode`, `late_s`
Example: `tool call reminders.fire '{"id": "1a2b3c4d", "at": "2026-10-24 09:00"}'`

## CLI and cron

`remind` (CLI for scripts and cron): `add "YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM" "text"` (my dated follow-ups) · `add --owner` (his) · `add --call` (rings him once via `voice-relay call`, then the usual text; any hour) · `add --on home|leave|zone:NAME|leave:zone:NAME [--every] [--urgent] [--tag T]` (place reminders, cron `remind place-due`; quiet hours held to 08:00 unless --urgent) · `when PHRASE [--json]` (EN/DE time or place phrases; add, snooze and move take them too) · `move N|ID WHEN` · `cancel/skip N|ID` (`cancel --tag T|--match TEXT`) · `list [--ids|--json [--mine]]` · `snooze ID WHEN` · `done ID` (both idempotent; series IDs refused: skip/cancel) · `buttons ID` + `press [DATA]` (the `rm:` reminder buttons) · `actions ID` + `act VERB ARG` (phone app) · `due` (the due pass: run by the hub timers, `hub tick` and `hub fallback`, never by cron) · `hub status|sync [--full]|tick|fire ID AT|fallback`. IDs are 8-hex; owner reminders go out via `tg-buttons`, then `tg`, then a note for me (typed `note.reminder*` event). Managed. Flags, env and internals: `remind --help`.
