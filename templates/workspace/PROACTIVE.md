# Proactive log
One line per finished item for the owner (heartbeat work, goal steps, builds, orders, passes the skills log here),
newest at the bottom: `- YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM — what — outcome (messaged owner? y/n)`. Counts and outcomes only, nothing
private. Changes to the assistant itself go to CHANGELOG.md instead. Read by night-log, the weekly digest's
"Fixed for you" section and the idea scan.
